    Upcoming payments

    Every subscription and bill you've listed, with the next due date. The bell warns you a few days before the bigger ones, and annual renewals also send an email.

    Is it in your plan?

    Each bill belongs to a line in your Monthly plan. If the bills cost more than the line, the plan is short and the difference comes out of guilt-free money without you noticing.

    All bills

    Can I afford it?

    Enter what you want to buy. It checks this month's guilt-free money first, then savings above your safety buffer. It never counts money set aside for bills, debt, investments or goals.

    Check a rental

    Enter a listing's weekly rent. It shows what moving in costs up front and what your plan looks like with that rent instead of today's.

    Unexpected bill

    A car repair, a dentist, a broken laptop. Enter it before you reach for the credit card. It covers the bill from the bottom of your priority list up, and never touches tax.

    Money goals

    A goal is money set aside each month for one thing. Set a target, a monthly amount and, if you like, a date. The emergency fund updates from your check-ins.

    If money is short, fund in this order

    Decided in advance, so a bad month doesn't decide for you. When an unexpected cost or a missed pay hits, cut from the bottom up.

      Your accounts

      What each ANZ account is for. Only accounts ticked “Buffer” count as your safety buffer; money set aside for goals or bills doesn't. Investment accounts count toward net worth, not cash. KiwiSaver is locked, so it can never be buffer. Credit cards subtract what you owe.

      Monthly plan Edit any amount

      Each column is a plan that starts in a given month and runs until the next one. Guilt-free money is whatever is left, so every change shows what it costs. Add a column when something changes, like a pay rise or new rent.
