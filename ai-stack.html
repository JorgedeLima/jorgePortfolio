How I keep control of the outcome

  1. 1

    Every claim needs a traceable source.

    If AI-drafted copy states a number or a fact, it has to point back to something real — a document, a screenshot, a transcript — before it ships. If it can't, it gets flagged and either verified or cut.

  2. 2

    Unverifiable output gets rejected and rebuilt, not polished.

    AI tools occasionally fill a gap with something plausible-sounding rather than something true. When that happens, the fix is tracing back to what's actually real and starting again from there, not softening the language and keeping it.

  3. 3

    One source of truth, not four drifting versions.

    Resume, LinkedIn and portfolio copy are tracked against a single working document, so a phrase or a number doesn't quietly diverge across channels over months of edits.

  4. 4

    Technical claims get tested, not taken on faith.

    A tool reporting something as "fixed" isn't the same as it being fixed. Claims like that get verified directly, in the browser, with real interaction, before they're marked done.
