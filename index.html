Senior Product Designer Hypothesis-led

I design with hypotheses, not assumptions.

I write the bet down before I design it, then test whether it held. Research, engineering and product are in the room throughout, because the insights that matter usually surface in that overlap.

How I work

01 — SEQUENCING

Fix it before you rebuild it.

On Booking.com for Business I repaired what we could inside the legacy system first and tested each change, so the full redesign was a decision backed by evidence rather than a leap.

02 — SCOPE

Descope to the decision.

Unleashed's dashboard was an operational data dump. I cut it down to what teams actually needed to make a faster call, and nothing else.

03 — OVERLAP

Work in the overlap, not after it.

Researchers, engineers and PMs throughout the project. Twenty-plus moderated sessions, AI-built prototypes, MVPs run in parallel with the live control.
